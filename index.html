耳石教学模拟

一款完全离线运行的 BPPV（良性阵发性位置性眩晕）医学教育 App，用于学习前庭解剖、位置试验与复位过程。

An offline medical education app for learning about benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV), vestibular anatomy, positional tests and repositioning maneuvers.

教学内容 / Educational content

前庭与三对半规管解剖、BPPV 理论知识、位置试验教学、复位手法三维模拟，以及眼震教学展示。

Vestibular and semicircular canal anatomy, BPPV theory, positional test demonstrations, 3D simulations of repositioning maneuvers, and educational nystagmus examples.

无需注册或登录，无广告。App 的核心教学内容在设备上离线运行。

No registration, sign-in or advertising. The app's core educational content works offline on your device.

本 App 仅供医学教育与学习，不能替代专业医疗诊断、个体化医疗建议或治疗。

For medical education and learning only. This app does not replace professional medical diagnosis, individual medical advice or treatment.
